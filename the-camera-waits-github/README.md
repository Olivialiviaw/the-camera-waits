# THE CAMERA WAITS

**Animals Under Observation**

An interactive camera-trap archive about looking, recording, classification, and the reversal of the gaze.

Move through the image to search the frame. Click to record four encounters: an empty field, a passing body, a returned gaze, and the moment when the observer becomes the observed.

Created by Olivia Tang as a thesis prototype at Parsons School of Design, 2026.
